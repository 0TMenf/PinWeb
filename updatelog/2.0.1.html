2.0.1 Update

+ Add 8 Language Support

PinWeb 2.0.1 added support for 8 new languages, They are: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, English, French, German, and Spanish.

+ Better Code/File Name

In the past, the naming of PinWeb's code and files was often arbitrary and poor.

Now, Taking advantage of multilingual support, we were able to organize these messy codes and file names.
